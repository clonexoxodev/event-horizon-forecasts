## Color Psychology

### BEFORE
- 🔴 **Red:** Used for losses, down arrows, negative ROI
- 🟢 **Green:** Used for wins, up arrows
- ⚫ **Gray:** Minimal use

**Problem:** Heavy use of red creates negative emotions

### AFTER
- 🟣 **Purple:** Balance (brand color, trust)
- 🔵 **Blue:** Active forecasts (engagement, calm)
- 🟢 **Green:** Accuracy (growth, achievement)
- 🟠 **Amber:** Portfolio (value, warmth)
- 🟣 **Indigo:** Watchlist (planning, wisdom)
- 🟡 **Yellow:** Points (energy, positivity)
- ⚫ **Gray:** Neutral outcomes (professional)

**Benefit:** Balanced, professional color scheme with no aggressive red

## User Psychology Impact

### BEFORE: Discouraging Experience
```
User opens dashboard
  ↓
Sees "Net Profit: -₦50K" in RED
  ↓
Sees "5 wins · 15 losses"
  ↓
Sees every loss with -₦X in RED
  ↓
Feels bad about losses
  ↓
May quit platform
```

### AFTER: Encouraging Experience
```
User opens dashboard
  ↓
Sees "Balance: ₦100K" (what they have)
  ↓
Sees "Active Forecasts: 3" (engagement)
  ↓
Sees "Accuracy: 25%" (room to improve)
  ↓
Sees "Points: 90" (gamification)
  ↓
Sees wins highlighted, losses neutral
  ↓
Feels encouraged to continue
  ↓
Stays engaged with platform
```
